How to Play

WASD move · Shift sprint
C sneak · Space dodge roll
V shove · E on a table: flip it for cover
Click attack · Right-click hold to aim
R reload · H quick heal
E search, open, use
Tab inventory · M map · Esc pause
1–6 hotbar · Wheel weapons
F flashlight · B barricade door
G Noise Maker · X Pipe Bomb
Q hold at a door or window to peek, silently

In the inventory, hover an item for details, right-click it for actions, and drag it anywhere: onto a gun, a gear slot, the hotbar, or outside the window to drop it.

Bring loot to the Stash in the fenced Safehouse and build upgrades at the Workbench. Zombies can't get in. Noise draws them. If you die, your gear stays on your body, marked on the map.
