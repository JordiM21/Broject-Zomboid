How to Play

WASD move
Shift sprint (loud)
Mouse aim · Click attack
Space shove zombies back
E search / open / use
Tab or I inventory
1–6 use hotbar slot (weapons equip / holster, items get used) · wheel cycle weapons
R reload
H quick heal
C sneak: quieter, harder to see. Hit unaware zombies from behind for a silent kill
F flashlight
M map
G throw Noise Maker
X throw Pipe Bomb
B barricade a closed door (plank + 3 nails)
E on a window: open it quietly, then climb through
Attack a door or window to smash it (loud)
Locked doors: pry with a Crowbar or smash them
Climbing through broken glass can cut you
Inventory: hover an item, then E use · Q drop · T move
R take all · Shift+R put all · Right-click item menu
Drag an item out of the inventory window to drop it
Hotbar: drag items onto it, drag slots to reorder, right-click or drag off to clear. In the inventory, hover an item and press 1–6 to assign
Doors and windows glow when you can use them
Esc pause

Your Safehouse is the fenced compound in the middle of town. Zombies can't enter it. Everything else is theirs.

Loot houses, stores, cars and the police station. Bring materials back and put them in the Stash to build upgrades at the Workbench.

If you die, everything you carried stays on your body, marked ✕ on the map. Go get it back.

Noise draws zombies. Gunshots carry far. Closed doors slow them down. Nights are darker and deadlier, and hordes roam from Day 2.
