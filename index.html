How to Play

WASD move · Shift sprint
C sneak · Space dodge roll
V shove · E on a table: flip it for cover
Click attack · Right-click hold to aim
R reload · H quick heal
E search, open, use
Tab inventory · M map · Esc pause
1–9 hotbar · Wheel weapons
F flashlight · B barricade door
Hold E on a downed partner to revive
Downed? Crawl to your partner or the green smoke
G Noise Maker · X Pipe Bomb
Q hold at a door or window to peek, silently

The inventory works like Minecraft: click a slot to pick up its stack, click another to put it down (drop a mod on a gun to attach it). Right-click picks up half or puts down one, Shift-click sends a stack across, and 1–9 over an item swaps it with that hotbar slot. Click the world to drop what you hold.

The Outpost is safe: stash your loot, build and repair at the Workbench, display guns. Board the bus to raid a fresh city with a drop crate of weapons. You have 15 minutes before overtime makes the dead faster, harder and endless. Reach the green smoke to extract. Die out there and you lose what you carried.
