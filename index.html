LAST HAUL

Scavenge the dead town. Haul it home. Don't die with it on you.

v0.1 · single-player · progress saves in this browser

How to Play

WASD move
Shift sprint (loud)
Mouse aim · Click attack
Space shove zombies back
E search / open / use
Tab or I inventory
1–6 / wheel switch weapon
R reload
H quick heal
C toggle sneak
F flashlight
M map
G throw Noise Maker
X throw Pipe Bomb

Your Safehouse is the fenced compound in the middle of town. Zombies can't enter it. Everything else is theirs.

Loot houses, stores, cars and the police station. Bring materials back and put them in the Stash to build upgrades at the Workbench.

If you die, everything you carried stays on your body, marked ✕ on the map. Go get it back.

Noise draws zombies. Gunshots carry far. Closed doors slow them down. Nights are darker and deadlier, and hordes roam from Day 2.
